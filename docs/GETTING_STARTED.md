# Getting Started

Welcome to RetroChat. This guide takes you from download to your first chat.

## 1. Install

- **iOS**: get the app from the [App Store](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/retro-chat-on-sol/id6766412897).
- **Desktop / Android**: visit [retrochat.io](https://retrochat.io) in your browser and install the PWA (or use the site directly).

See [Install RetroChat](INSTALL.md) for full instructions.

## 2. Sign in or sign up

Choose a sign-in method:

- **Email & password** — enter an email and password, then confirm you are 16+.
- **Apple** or **Google** — continue with your Apple or Google account.
- **Wallet** — connect Phantom, or let RetroChat create an in-app wallet for you (18+ attestation for wallet generation).
- **X (Twitter)** — sign in with your X account.

See [Authentication](AUTHENTICATION.md) for details.

## 3. Set up your profile

- Pick a screen name (or generate one).
- Add an avatar and short bio.
- Confirm your age attestation if prompted.

## 4. Take the tour

The onboarding tour highlights the tabs you'll use most:

- **Rooms** for real-time chat.
- **Communities** and **Feed** for longer-form posts.
- **Wallet** for tips, trades, and on-chain assets.
- **Live** for community streams and Spaces.
- **Radio** for background internet radio.
- **Retro TV** for video channels.
- **Token Studio** / **Learn** when you are ready to explore launches or help articles.

## 5. Join or create your first room

Tap **Rooms**, browse featured rooms, and join one that looks fun — or tap **Create Room** to make your own. See [Chat Rooms](CHAT_ROOMS.md).

## 6. Follow a few people and communities

Visit any profile and tap **Follow**. Their posts appear in your [Feed](COMMUNITIES_AND_FEED.md).

## 7. (Optional) Fund your wallet

To tip creators or trade tokens, add some SOL:

- Send from an external wallet using your address in **Wallet → Receive**, or
- Buy with a card via **Wallet → Buy Crypto** (regional availability).

See the [Wallet Guide](WALLET_GUIDE.md).
